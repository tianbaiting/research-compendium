\begin{abstract}
This dossier organizes the inference problem for the triton--triton ($t+t$) system: which experimentally accessible quantities constrain its strong interaction, which interpretations depend on Coulomb conventions or coupled-channel dynamics, and what calculations are needed for reproducible comparisons. It is a \emph{working framework}, not yet an exhaustive inventory of experimental measurements. In particular, no unverified numerical scattering length or effective range is reported.
\end{abstract}
