\section{Provisional conclusions and research agenda}\label{sec:tt-conclusion}
At present the defensible conclusion is a \emph{methodological} one: any claimed constraint on the triton--triton potential must identify an observable, a channel convention, a Coulomb treatment, an energy range, and a validated inference map. A list of resonances or cross sections alone is not an interaction constraint.

\subsection{Next verifiable deliverables}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Build a DOI/archival-link-checked chronology of direct $t+t$ scattering experiments.
\item Establish an evidence matrix separating direct measurements, secondary phase-shift analyses, and microscopic calculations.
\item Reconstruct the Coulomb conventions and threshold definitions in each extraction.
\item Compare published constraints under consistent uncertainties and specify remaining degeneracies.
\end{enumerate}
