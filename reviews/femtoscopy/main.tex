\begin{abstract}
A correlation measurement is a convolution of production geometry, two-particle propagation, quantum statistics, and detector/reconstruction effects. This working review establishes a notation-first framework for determining which interaction parameters are identifiable after source-model and detector uncertainties are accounted for.
\end{abstract}
